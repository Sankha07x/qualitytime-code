Simple 4-Step Android Installation Guide

Installing an APK file directly is 100% safe, fast, and takes less than 30 seconds.

1

Download the APK

Tap the green "Download APK" button above. Your browser (Chrome, Samsung Internet, Firefox) will start saving the official release file.

2
